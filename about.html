A little about me

Hi! I’m Anastasiia (stacynorth), a Ukrainian self-taught children’s book illustrator and aspiring author.

I worked as an illustrator and animator for Suspilne Ukraine, the country’s largest public broadcaster, creating illustrations and animations for children’s fairy tales. My freelance experience includes book illustration, character design, posters, postcards and editorial artwork.

I create charming characters and cozy, imaginative worlds, helping authors bring their children’s stories to life. I’d love to illustrate your next book!
